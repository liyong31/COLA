\subsection{Pruning between independent SCC constructions}
Each SCC retains its own ranked-run construction and shifted original
Emerson--Lei acceptance. A separate certified coverage step removes a run
$q$ only if its language is included in the union of retained runs in
strictly lower SCCs. SCC numbers use reverse topological order.
Processing SCCs in increasing order ensures that covering runs are retained;
local rank numbers are never compared between SCCs.

For an accepted suffix, a language-union certificate supplies an accepting
continuation from one covering run. Every subsequent cross-SCC replacement
or proper SCC transition strictly decreases the SCC number. Thus an accepting
witness cannot be replaced infinitely often. After the last replacement,
the existing local construction preserves its accepting continuation.
Within-SCC coverage retains its older-run rule and rank discontinuation
acceptance is unchanged. Unknown annotations cannot justify deletion.
This is a sufficient, deliberately conservative coverage policy.

Identical-input ablations cover 1816 outputs over both polarities and both
OWL algorithm families. Positive paired successes contain six improvements,
443 ties and no increases, saving 88 states. Negative paired successes
contain eleven improvements, 428 ties and six increases, saving 220 states.
A negative SEJK16 acceptance-capacity failure also recovers. These counts
exclude explicit resource failures from paired state totals.
Fewer tracked runs do not imply fewer reachable macrostates: negative EKS20
Hardness~1 grows from 22 to 29 states, and Hardness~3 from 24 to 60.
The optional LTL route therefore compares the pruned and original SCC
constructions on the same input and retains the smaller checked result.

OWL's safety scheduler is a different optimisation. For
$FGa\lor FGb\lor FGc\lor FGd\lor FGe$, the exported accepting singleton
languages $Ga,Gb,Gc,Gd,Ge$ are incomparable. Our weak subset bookkeeping
produces 32 states, while OWL schedules one safety guess at a time and
produces five. The gap persists when OWL's polarity and fragment portfolios
are disabled. Coverage alone cannot delete these incomparable guesses;
a sound scheduler needs an additional restartability/fairness certificate.
The implementation does not yet supply that certificate, nor a global
singly exponential language-basis bound across independent SCCs.
